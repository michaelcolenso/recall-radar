## Not changed: recommendations that need your decision

1. **Risk grades barely separate vehicles.** Of 80 random year pages, 67 show a grade in the title: 28 F (42%), 19 D (28%), 3 C, 11 B and 6 A. A 2020 Camry with 3 recalls is rated "F" because two of them are classed CRITICAL. With most vehicles in D or F, the grade doesn't tell vehicles apart. Putting "Grade F" in search titles is also a credibility risk for the site and invites complaints from brands. **Options:** keep grades off titles, or grade relative to peers. This is a methodology decision, so I didn't change it.
2. **Sitemap `lastmod` is the ingestion time, not the time the content changed.** Weekly re-ingestion moves `lastmod` forward even when no filing changed. Google ignores `lastmod` from sites where it proves unreliable. Each page's `dateModified` and its "Updated" text use the same timestamp on purpose, and existing tests cover that. Any fix should change all three to the newest filing or enrichment date together.
3. **The sitemap is a single 5.9 MB file** with 32,279 URLs, and a cold fetch took 2.5 s. That is under the 50,000-URL limit, and the code switches to an index automatically above it, so this is not urgent.
4. **404 responses carry `s-maxage=43200`.** A model that 404s before its first ingestion can stay cached as missing for up to 12 hours.
5. **The home page meta description hardcodes "16K+ recalls across 30 makes".** It will drift out of date as data grows.

## Rollout

This change is not deployed yet. It adds no migrations and needs no data repair. After `npm run deploy`:

```sh
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code}\n" https://recalledrides.com/toyota/air-bags-recalls      # expect 200
curl -s -o /dev/null -w "%{http_code} %{redirect_url}\n" https://recalledrides.com/TOYOTA/camry  # expect 301 → /toyota/camry
curl -s https://recalledrides.com/toyota/camry/2020 | grep -o "<title>[^<]*"                     # expect "2020 Toyota Camry …"
```

Then re-check every `-recalls` URL in the sitemap for a 200 response.
